There is no minimum on stocked items — buy one pair of gloves if that is what you need. Branded goods carry a minimum: 36 rolls for printed tape, 10 pieces for embroidered shirts, 25 pieces for printed t-shirts. Each product page lists its own MOQ.

Yes, to all 47 counties. Nairobi is delivered by our own vehicles within 24 hours; upcountry goes by courier overnight with tracking. Courier charges are shown on the quotation before you approve it.

Vector is best — AI, EPS or PDF with fonts outlined. A high-resolution PNG usually works for print. If all you have is a logo from a letterhead, our studio will redraw it for free and send it back to you to keep.
